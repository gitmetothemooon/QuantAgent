# QuantAgent Research Log

This document records the reasoning behind every quantitative decision made during the development of QuantAgent.

The objective is to ensure every indicator, strategy, and model exists because it improves the system—not because it is popular.

---

## Evaluation Principles

Every indicator must answer five questions:

1. What does it measure?

2. Why should it improve investment decisions?

3. Where does it fail?

4. How much influence should it have?

5. Did backtesting justify keeping it?
